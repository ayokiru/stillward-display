# Changelog

Every release of Stillward Display, newest first. Builds arrive through Steam,
and each one is also a [release](https://github.com/ayokiru/stillward-display/releases)
here: choose Watch → Custom → Releases to hear about them.

## Unreleased

Stillward Display is in final testing ahead of its Steam release.
[Wishlist it](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5248400/Stillward_Display/) to
hear the day it comes out. The first release brings:

- Window animations for every moment of a window's life, from the Steam
  Workshop, with an editor, a grid to draw a movement of your own, and styles
  as files.
- A taskbar and a dock of its own, Apps with search, a control centre you lay
  out yourself, and taskbar looks from the Workshop.
- The island: what is playing, timers, the clipboard, a file shelf, and
  activities from the Workshop.
- Pointer sets, a pointer studio and rings at every click.
- Widgets on the wallpaper and a widget studio.
- Icon sets for the dock, the taskbar and the desktop.
- Setups: a whole look in one item, shared by link or code.
- Windows as tabs, a scrolling strip of windows, every window side by side,
  rules for where programs open, zones under Shift, outlines and frames.
- Screenshots with marks, secrets that cover themselves, recordings that zoom
  where you click, and the last minutes saved on a key.
- Presets and desks: windows on their monitors, the sound output, the display
  mode and the rest set up once and put back when you are done.
- 30 languages, Steam achievements and Steam Cloud.
